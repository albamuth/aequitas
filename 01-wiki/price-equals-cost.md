# Price ≡ Cost

> The price of anything is its true, current-best-estimate material cost. There is no profit in exchange — only debit discharged and debit acquired.

## What it is

Axiom A5. A transaction is not a transfer of value from one party to another with a spread taken in the middle. It is a **reassignment of debit**: the seller's [[property-debit]] for the item is released, and the buyer takes it on.

Competition does not disappear — it moves. Producers compete on:
- **quality**
- **artfulness**
- **efficiency** (i.e. genuinely lower material cost, which is real)

They cannot compete on margin, because there is no margin.

## Why it works this way

Profit in exchange is the mechanism by which capital compounds. Remove it and compounding capital has no source — see [[capitalism-cannot-function]]. It is not banned by rule; there is simply no field in the record where it could be written.

Important framing point: A5 does **not** abolish markets. It abolishes what [[markets-vs-antimarkets|DeLanda calls the antimarket]] — the price-*setting* firm large enough to impose prices from above rather than take them from below. Small-producer markets, which set price by cost anyway under real competition, survive intact. **Aequitas is pro-market and anti-capitalist, and this page is where that claim is grounded.**
