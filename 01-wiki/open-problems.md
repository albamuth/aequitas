# Open problems OP-1 to OP-28

> **Version:** 0.1
> **Date:** 2026-10-14

> **Every open problem the project has registered, with its status as the Objections register's status board gives it.** An agent answering a question that lands on a row marked 🔴 or 🟠 says the problem is open and cites the row. It does not invent an answer. **The register is the source; this page is the index.**

## The words this page uses

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| 🔴 | Open and blocking. |
| 🟠 | Open, with partial answers or a mechanism in place whose effect is unmeasured. |
| 🔽 | Stated, not worked. Minor or deferred. |
| ✅ | Closed, dissolved, resolved or answered. Nothing owed. |

**The pattern the register notes.** OP-17, OP-11, OP-21 and OP-23 closed by *removing* a division. OP-18 closed by *declaring* a convention where the trace test mandates one. The credit-realisation cluster closed when debit-follows-possession turned out to invert the attacker's leverage. **And the honest outcome of a stress test is sometimes "this is the old hole wearing a new hat"**, which keeps the count from inflating.
