# OP-16 — the onerousness gap

> **Version:** 0.1
> **Date:** 2026-10-14

> **🟠 Open. Both halves are now reachable, and neither is measured.** A2 refuses to rate-scale labour, so a dull job and a pleasant job credit the same per hour. **A pledge can name whoever does the dull job, so society can simply pledge more for work nobody wants.** Whether enough people will is a behavioural question nobody has tested.

## The words this page uses

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Rate-scaling** | Crediting one hour as more than one hour because of skill, rank or hazard. Forbidden by A2 ([time-as-yardstick](time-as-yardstick.md)). |
| **Causal tail** | A later, measurable harm a job causes, which the accounting can inject back as debit. Hazard has one; tedium does not. |
| **Pledge** | A permanent grant of debit-room, spent once from the pledger's budget of earned credit ([pledge-and-signal](pledge-and-signal.md)). |

## The problem

**Parecon remunerates duration, intensity and onerousness. Aequitas resolves differences materially instead.** Exertion lands as extra food, hazard as a health harm injected back into the products the process made, skill as training credited and carried when it happens ([education-as-credited-work](education-as-credited-work.md)). **What is left is disutility with no material signature and no causal tail**: tedium, isolation, indignity. Dull-but-safe work causes no future cost, so nothing in the accounting reaches it.

**This is not theoretical.** Time banking credits every hour identically, which is A2's flat hour, and 45 years across dozens of countries produces chronic skill mismatch and skills in short supply ([field-record](field-record.md), [cost-the-limit-of-price](cost-the-limit-of-price.md)).

## What is settled

- **Skill is answered** by front-loaded training (A2 v0.3).
- **Hazard is answered** by retroactive harm injection, and since 2026-09-18 by pledges whose criteria name *whoever mitigates harm from this task*.
- **Tedium is reachable**, since the same ruling: a pledge may simply name whoever does the dull job. The withdrawn contingent reserve could not reach it, because only hazard leaves a causal tail for cover to attach to. **A2 is untouched: the worker is still credited one hour per hour; what changes is how much room other people choose to grant them, which is demand deciding rather than a rate.**

## An example, with the numbers

**A sewer shift and a library shift each take 8 hours, the same calories, the same training, and no later harm.** Each credits **8 hours** and nothing in the accounting tells them apart.

| | Pledged room per shift | Who does it |
|---|--:|---|
| The library shift, which people want | 0 h | Someone, because they like it |
| The sewer shift, which nobody wants | 200 people pledge 0.1 h each = **20 h** | Whoever takes the 8 hours of credit and the 20 hours of room |

**In plain words: the hourly credit did not move, and the room did.** What is unmeasured is whether 200 people actually pledge, and what happens in a community where they do not.

## Candidates still on the table

- **Check how much of the gap is unmeasured hazard.** Night shift, isolation and repetitive strain have documented health costs the hazard clause already injects. **Do this first.** The test: compute the debit-cost of a night-shift cleaner, a day-shift cleaner and a rural postal carrier; if the figures come out identical, the gap is confirmed.
- **Hour-ceiling differentiation**: a sustainable sewer shift is 4 hours, a pleasant one 8; both credit one hour per hour. Weakness: who sets the ceilings lands in [op-10](op-10.md).
- **Automation pressure**: unfilled pledges are a quantified signal saying *automate this*.
- **Rotation**, last resort, carrying the compulsory-labour critique.
- **Withdrawn**: *route onerous work to service credit.* There is no separate service credit to pay a premium in ([service-credit](service-credit.md)).
