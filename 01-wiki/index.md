## The axioms

| # | Page | One line |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | [material-flow-value](material-flow-value.md) | Credit and debit are records of matter and energy moving. |
| A2 | [time-as-yardstick](time-as-yardstick.md) | Time measures flows; it is not itself value. Labor is never rate-scaled. |
| A3 | [non-fungibility](non-fungibility.md) | Credits are unique event records. Only debit moves. |

## Credit and feedback

**There is one credit, time spent.** Production, service and enrichment are the three names that decide *whether* an hour counts, never *how much*. Feedback (likes, applause, referrals) was never credit and does not convert into it.
