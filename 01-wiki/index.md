## Credit and feedback

> ⚠️ **These three pages are stale.** Foundations v0.3 restructured §6: there are **not** three credit types. There is **one credit — time worked** — and three *feedback channels*. An apprentice plumber's hour is production, service and enrichment at once and cannot be partitioned.
