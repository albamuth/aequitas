# The front-loading rule

> **Version:** 0.1
> **Date:** 2026-10-14

> **A large up-front cost with a diffuse benefit is carried where it is incurred, and cushioned at that time by the backing of the people who pledged for it. It is never charged onward to whoever happens to consume the result.**

## The words this page uses

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pledge** | Permanent backing of a named person, spent once from the pledger's lifetime budget of earned credit ([pledge-and-signal](pledge-and-signal.md)). |
| **Creation cost** | The hours and materials that made a durable thing. It sits on the thing as property debit ([property-debit](property-debit.md)). |
| **Holding time** | How long a person held a thing. A physical trace, so a split by it is measured rather than invented ([physical-trace-test](physical-trace-test.md)). |
| **Capital** | A thing that survives the process it is used in. **Consumption** is what the process used up. |

## The rule

**It covers education, media and creative production, research, infrastructure, tooling, and capital.** Training is its first case: a student is credited for their time, the teachers and buildings are carried during the training years, and nothing of it ever appears in a patient's bill ([education-as-credited-work](education-as-credited-work.md)). A film's audience pays for delivery only.

**Two reasons, and the second is decisive.** Charging a cost onward means choosing a window, how many patients, how many viewers, and every candidate window is arbitrary. And charging onward never terminates: amortise a hospital into each bill and the accounting must chase the builder's costs, the steelmaker's, the engineers' schooling, back to the first human activity. **Carrying the cost where it falls is what makes the arithmetic stop.** This is the upstream face of the rule that pollution never flows downstream to a buyer ([pollution-making-vs-using](pollution-making-vs-using.md)): cost attaches only to the causer and never cascades.

**The boundary is capital against consumption, told apart by physical fate.** Did the thing survive the process? A drill bit that survives is capital. The oil it burned is consumption and reaches the unit. This is checked against the record of what happened to each thing (IC-4), never by the producer's declaration.

**Who carries a durable asset, in three steps.**

1. **Pledges back the holders, so that they may carry it.** A pledge does not shrink the creation cost; nothing may vanish (A1). A facility is built at the scale the community will pledge for, so pledges are both the authorisation and the brake.
2. **The full creation cost is shared among the asset's holders by holding time.** Each holder's permanent share is their holding time divided by the total holding time over the asset's whole life. **A new hire bears almost nothing**, which removes the entry toll an equal split would put on exactly the capital-heavy essential work society most needs staffed. A carrier in transit takes no share.
3. **The basic-needs floor caps how hard any remainder bites** ([debit-tolerance](debit-tolerance.md)).

## An example, with the numbers

**A hospital costs 100,000 hours to build. The community pledges 50,000 hours toward it. Over its 60-year life about 200 people staff it at any time.**

| | Hours |
|---|--:|
| Creation cost on the asset | **100,000 h**, and it stays 100,000 |
| Backing cushioning the holders | 50,000 h, so **50,000 h** of it effectively restricts anybody |
| A nurse hired on Monday | **≈ 0 h**, no holding time yet |
| A 30-year veteran's share, 30 ÷ (200 × 60) holder-years | **≈ 0.25%**, about **250 h** |

**Now the barn, the case people attack most.** A barn costs 20,000 hours and shelters cattle that yield 40,000 kg of beef over 20 years. The beef carries **0.0 h/kg** from the barn, not 0.5. The 20,000 hours sit on the barn's holders: at ρ = 1.2 that needs 16,667 hours of credit standing behind it, which is **4.6 years of one person's entire credit accrual** at 3,650 hours a year. **That is why nobody builds a barn they do not need**, and why pushing the cost onto 40,000 buyers who never had a say would free the one party who decided.

**In plain words: the up-front cost lands on whoever chose it and holds it, cushioned by whoever wanted it, and never on whoever later uses the result.**

## What it dissolved, and what it leaves

**Dissolved:** OP-11 (training amortisation), OP-5 (education cost) and OP-21 (media reproduction), which were one malformed question about a downstream window; and OP-23 (shared overhead), because capital accrues to the asset and never to co-products ([co-product-allocation](co-product-allocation.md)).

**Honest limits.** A first-time creator attracts no pledges; the path is small unpledged work, feedback, then pledges. And a per-unit figure is not a full-lifecycle figure: two producers with a 20,000-hour barn and a 2,000-hour shed publish the same number per kilogram. **What disciplines the barn is the builder's own gate, not the label on the beef.**
