# Cost, Not Price

> A thing's cost is the current best estimate of **what was materially consumed to make it**. Nothing is added to that figure, and nothing enters it that the thing did not consume.

**Axiom A5.** *Renamed and repaired in Foundations v0.21. It previously read "price ≡ cost", and that wording was found to contradict the capital rule — see [Why the old name was wrong](#why-the-old-name-was-wrong).*

## What it is

Two claims in one axiom, and they fail differently, so keep them apart:

| | The claim | What it rules out |
|---|---|---|
| **Nothing is added** | A transaction is not a transfer of value with a spread taken in the middle. It is a **reassignment of debit** — the seller's [property-debit](property-debit.md) is released, the buyer takes it on. | Margin, profit in exchange, markup, interest, fees. |
| **Nothing enters that the thing did not consume** | The boundary is **physical fate**: what was used up making the thing is in its cost; what survived the process is not. | Amortising a factory, a barn, or a tool into the things it was used to make. |

Competition does not disappear — it moves. Producers compete on **quality**, **artfulness**, and **efficiency** (genuinely lower material cost, which is real). They cannot compete on margin, because there is no margin.

**And the figure is never final.** Better measurement re-weighs it and every record made under it ([derived-ledger](derived-ledger.md), retroactive re-weighting). **A cost is a dated reading, not a verdict.**

## Why it works this way

**On the first half.** Profit in exchange is the mechanism by which capital compounds. Remove it and compounding capital has no source — see [capitalism-cannot-function](capitalism-cannot-function.md). It is not banned by rule; **there is simply no field in the record where it could be written.**

**On the second half, which is the part people argue with.** A barn shelters cattle for twenty years. Why is the barn not in the beef?

> **Because the beef did not build the barn.** Under [A1](material-flow-value.md) only people act, so a cost attaches to whoever caused it — and **a thing causes nothing.** Charging a beef buyer for the barn is the same error as charging a ring buyer for the miner's tailings, which [the non-transfer rule](no-externalities.md) already refuses.

**The numbers.** A 20,000-hour barn over 40,000 kg of beef would be **0.5 h/kg**. Aequitas shows **0.0**. The 20,000 hours sit on the farmer, and at ρ = 1.2 they need about **16,700 hours of credit** standing behind them — roughly **four and a half years** of one person's entire accrual. *That* is what stops a barn going up that nobody needs. Push it onto the beef instead and the cost leaves the one person who chose to build it and lands on ~40,000 buyers who did not. Full worked cases: Foundations §4.5.

**Important framing point:** A5 does **not** abolish markets. It abolishes what [DeLanda calls the antimarket](markets-vs-antimarkets.md) — the price-*setting* firm large enough to impose prices from above rather than take them from below. Small-producer markets, which set price by cost anyway under real competition, survive intact. **Aequitas is pro-market and anti-capitalist, and this page is where that claim is grounded.**

## Why the old name was wrong

The axiom read *"the **price** of anything is its true, current-best-estimate material cost"* until v0.21, when an outside economist review put that sentence against the capital rule and found a contradiction. **Three defects:**

1. **It said "price."** Nothing in Aequitas has a price. Things carry a **debit-cost**, and it moves.
2. **It never said what counts as a cost *of the thing*** — so the capital boundary, which lived elsewhere, read as contradicting the axiom.
3. **"True" reads as final**, which fights retroactive re-weighting and [derived-ledger](derived-ledger.md).

**The ruling was not the error; the axiom's wording was.** Nothing about how capital is carried changed. Register entry: Objections **B8**.

## Who games this

- **The capital launderer** — reclassify a used-up input as a durable asset to move its debit off the unit. **Closed:** the boundary is audited by fate closure, never declared by the producer.
- **Scarcity in costume** — charging more for the sought-after cut. **Closed, and this axiom is why:** cost is a record of what was consumed, so desirability has no way in. See [co-product-allocation](co-product-allocation.md).

## The honest residue

**Two producers of the same good — one with a big barn, one with a shed — publish the same per-unit debit-cost.** A buyer comparing debit-costs cannot tell them apart, because the figure answers *"what did this unit consume?"* and never *"what does this producer's method cost?"*

**What disciplines the barn is the builder's own ledger, not the sticker** — the same argument [no-externalities](no-externalities.md) already makes about pollution, where the debit likewise sits on the producer rather than the product, and where the producer-side penalty is **stronger** precisely because it does not depend on a shopper noticing.
