# Mutualism (Proudhon)

> Pierre-Joseph Proudhon's system: goods exchanged **at cost**, credit extended **without interest**, and rent and profit treated as extraction rather than payment for labor.

## The claim

From *What Is Property?* (1840) — "property is theft," but with a distinction Marx did not make: Proudhon separated **property** (holdings that generate income from others' labor) from **possession** (what you use and occupy). Only the former was theft.

Two load-bearing ideas:

1. **Just price = labor required to produce.** Exchange at cost, not at what the buyer will bear.
2. **Mutual credit.** Participants extend credit to one another without interest, replacing both state currency and bank intermediation — removing the rent taken by financial intermediaries. He attempted this concretely with the Banque du Peuple (1849), which failed within months.

Proudhon called capital "dead labor" — already paid, essentially unproductive — and opposed rent and interest as income categories that are not payment for labor.

## What Aequitas inherits

- **The diagnosis.** Rent and interest as extraction rather than compensation is exactly [capitalism-cannot-function](capitalism-cannot-function.md), reached 186 years earlier.
- **Cost-based exchange.** [cost-not-price](cost-not-price.md) is Proudhon's just price with the labor theory swapped for a material-flow theory ([material-flow-value](material-flow-value.md)).
- **The possession/property split** maps closely onto [property-debit](property-debit.md): you may hold and use, but holding carries burden rather than yield.

## Where Aequitas diverges — and why it matters

**Proudhon's remedy was monetary. Aequitas's is not.**

Mutual credit is still a *currency*: fungible, transferable, lendable. That means it can be accumulated and concentrated, and mutualist banking experiments consistently failed on exactly that plus the difficulty of clearing at scale. [non-fungibility](non-fungibility.md) is Aequitas's answer, and it is the sharpest single difference from the entire mutualist tradition.

Second divergence: Proudhon's just price rests on **labor-time**, which forces the skill/effort problem back in ([time-as-yardstick](time-as-yardstick.md) A2 exists precisely to escape it — see [cost-the-limit-of-price](cost-the-limit-of-price.md) for what happens when you don't).

## Note on Anarcho-Communism

*[The Conquest of Bread](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/petr-kropotkin-the-conquest-of-bread)* (1892) by **Kropotkin** argues for need-based distribution, which is a different and in places opposed position.
