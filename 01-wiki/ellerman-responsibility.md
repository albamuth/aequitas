# Ellerman's responsibility imputation

> **Version:** 0.1
> **Date:** 2026-10-14

> **A flow is attributed to whoever caused it, because responsibility is imputed to whoever was in fact responsible.** That is [David Ellerman's labour theory of property](http://et.worldeconomicsassociation.org/files/WEA-ET-5-1-Ellerman.pdf), and it is the principle courts already use for crimes, transplanted to production. **Only people act. A tool does not, a machine does not, and neither does whoever owns one.**

## The words this page uses

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Theory of value** | A claim about what things are worth. Marx's labour theory of value is one. Aequitas has none. |
| **Theory of imputation** | A claim about who did what. It says nothing about worth. |
| **Residual** | What is left of a process's product after inputs are paid. Under capitalism it goes to capital's owner; under Ellerman only the people who acted can claim it. |

## What it is

**A theory of value tries to say what things are worth. A theory of imputation only says who did what**, so it inherits none of the problems that sank the value theories, the transformation problem, Sraffa's negative values, a century of hostile priors, and it argues from a principle its opponents already accept.

**Three things it buys the theory** (Objections B6):

1. **A3 becomes a consequence rather than a design choice.** A credit records who was responsible, responsibility is a fact about a person, and facts about people do not change hands. There is nothing coherent to transfer ([non-fungibility](non-fungibility.md)).
2. **A second, independent route to "capitalism cannot function."** Only people act, so only people can be responsible, so capital cannot claim a residual ([capitalism-cannot-function](capitalism-cannot-function.md)).
3. **The reply to *"why do the machine's owners get nothing?"*** Because machines do not act.

**Take the principle, decline the institution.** Ellerman draws institutional conclusions about worker ownership that Aequitas does not adopt; it keeps functional institutions and changes only their economic nature.

**The cost of adopting him** was his own argument that joint responsibility is non-decomposable, which became OP-18. It closed in two halves: team *credit* dissolves, because each member is credited their own hours, and team *debit* divides by hours worked as a declared convention ([organisation-debit](organisation-debit.md)).

## An example, with the numbers

**A gold mine produces 1 kg of gold and 10 tonnes of tailings. A jeweller makes a ring from 5 g of it, and a buyer wears it.** Remediating a tonne of tailings is read at 8 hours (illustrative).

| | Who acted | What the ledger records |
|---|---|---|
| The 10 t of tailings, 80 h | The miners | **80 h of pollution debit on the miners, permanently**, divided by hours worked |
| The 5 g of gold in the ring | — | Property debit that **moves with the ring** to the jeweller, then the buyer |
| The buyer's share of the tailings | They did not mine | **0 h.** Charging them would misattribute responsibility |
| The mine's owner, who did not work there | They did not act | **0 h of credit.** Credit records who was responsible |

**In plain words: the tailings stay on the people whose hands were on the process, the gold travels with the ring, and the owner of the drill is credited nothing for owning it** ([pollution-making-vs-using](pollution-making-vs-using.md)). **Cost never flows to whoever did not cause it**, neither downstream to a buyer nor upstream to the first human who ever built anything ([front-loading-rule](front-loading-rule.md)).

## Also banked from the same adoption

**Tractability.** [Cockshott and Cottrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towards_a_New_Socialism) showed in-kind calculation at national scale runs with sparse-matrix methods. Cite them rather than re-proving it ([calculation-in-kind](calculation-in-kind.md)).
