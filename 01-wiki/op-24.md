# OP-24 — understatement drift

> **Version:** 0.1
> **Date:** 2026-10-14

> **🔴 Open. The proposed fix was withdrawn on 2026-08-24.** Errors that favour subscribers have no funder. **Auditing cost constants is a trust-network design problem held to five published requirements, and a network with no answer is not conforming.** Nobody has yet shown an answer that works, because no network exists.

## The words this page uses

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Cost constant** | A published number saying what a process takes, such as the hours to remediate a tonne of CO₂. |
| **Re-weighting** | When a constant improves, every record computed from it recalculates backwards ([retroactive-reweighting](retroactive-reweighting.md)). |
| **Extent** | How much of the world the books cover. **Weight** is what a recorded flow costs. The two audits are in different shape. |

## The problem

| Error direction | Who wants it fixed | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| The constant **overstates** a debit | Everyone paying it | Corrected quickly |
| The constant **understates** a debit | Nobody. Correcting it worsens every subscriber's ledger | **Nothing happens** |

**The result is systematic drift toward under-costing.** It is the observed failure mode of every carbon-accounting regime attempted so far, and the books tolerate it arithmetically because no global balance is required ([books-never-balance](books-never-balance.md)). **No equation breaks.** It erodes A4 quietly. The largest levers, the natural-remediation baseline and the ambient-stock reading, are constants with no rival at all: everyone benefits from a high baseline and a low stock.

**One channel is closed for free.** There is no market-dominating firm to fund a favourable result, because A5 removes the profit that pays for captured science today. **That is real and it is not sufficient.**

**Why the rival-sector fix was withdrawn.** It said plant-protein producers would fund the replication of an understated beef constant. Rivals are often absent, and a rival's best move is not to fund your correction but to get their own constant set generously: funding a replication costs real hours and the benefit is shared with every other rival, while a generous own-constant is cheap and private. **The equilibrium is mutual understatement, not mutual policing** ([rival-sector-audit](rival-sector-audit.md)).

## What is settled

- **The requirement stays in Foundations even though the mechanism does not.** Five properties any answer must have: two unaffiliated replications before a re-weight; method, version and uncertainty interval on every constant; triage by magnitude times concentration of beneficiary; public membership composition; a statement of what has not been reviewed and how old each reading is (§3.3a; conformance 16a to 16c).
- **The audit of extent has interested parties; the audit of weight does not.** An instrumented producer is harmed when undocumented produce prices too cheaply, and a dark producer cannot transact until they join ([unassigned-residual](unassigned-residual.md)). That asymmetry is the whole of the relief.
- **"Funding" is not the problem.** Audit work is credited work and recording is never gated, so the credit was never scarce. **What OP-24 is about is demand**: someone must still want the correction.

## An example, with the numbers

**A network reviews any constant whose effect exceeds 5,000 hours across its books.**

| | Total effect | Who benefits | Reviewed on size alone? |
|---|--:|---|---|
| Constant A, understated | 6,000 h | spread across 300,000 subscribers | **Yes** |
| Constant B, understated | 4,000 h | **one producer** | **No** |

**An attacker's job under a size-only rule is to keep each falsification just under the threshold.** Multiplying by concentration of beneficiary fixes it: B's 4,000 hours on one party outranks A's 6,000 across 300,000. That is requirement 3, and it is why the requirements are stated rather than left to good faith.

## What would settle it

**The test has not been run:** simulate a population of trust networks under these incentives and find the conditions under which drift stops being arrested (Objections §C, item 2). **Watch item recorded by the register:** this is the third governance question scoped to trust networks, and the first whose scope-out comes without a demonstration that the accounting is unaffected, because drift genuinely damages the books.
