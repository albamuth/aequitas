# Production Credit

> ⚠️ **STALE — DO NOT LEARN THE SYSTEM FROM THIS PAGE.** It describes the pre-v0.3 *three credit types* model, which Foundations replaced. There is **one credit — time worked** — and production/service/enrichment are three *feedback channels*, not credit types, with no accounting boundary between them (Foundations §6). This page is kept only until it is rewritten (queued in `NEXT.md`); read [index](index.md) and Foundations §6 instead.

> Credit for making material things. Its function is to **raise the debit ceiling** — how much you may hold and consume.

## What it is

The first of three credit types. Production credit is the most straightforwardly material of the three: you caused matter and energy to become more useful, and the record says so.

It governs capacity, not purchasing power. There is no price to pay ([cost-not-price](cost-not-price.md)) and nothing to spend ([non-fungibility](non-fungibility.md)) — what production credit does is expand the amount of [property-debit](property-debit.md) and [consumption-debit](consumption-debit.md) your account can carry in good standing.

## Why it works this way

The ceiling framing is what keeps credit from behaving like money. Money is *spent down*; a ceiling is *lived under*. You cannot transfer a ceiling, hoard it usefully beyond your own consumption, or lend it.

It also gives the right shape to the basic-needs question: everyone gets a floor by [debit-tolerance](debit-tolerance.md) regardless of production, and production credit raises the ceiling above that floor.

## Relation to the other two

| Type | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Production** | How much may I hold and consume? |
| [service-credit](service-credit.md) | Who gets to direct public effort? |
| [enrichment](enrichment.md) | What should we collectively do more of? |

The three must not be inter-convertible, or the system collapses to one currency (OP-8).

## Open questions

- Does the ceiling decay, reset, or accumulate for life? An accumulating lifetime ceiling looks a great deal like wealth. **Unspecified and load-bearing.**
- How does production credit interact with [self-work-identity](self-work-identity.md)? If self-work nets to zero, it should raise no ceiling — worth confirming explicitly.
