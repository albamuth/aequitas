# Consumption Debit

> Debit for matter and energy you consumed or degraded rather than hold. It is a *permanent-history* term: locked into the record forever and never discharged — but its **weight floats**.

## What it is

Food eaten, fuel burned, road surface worn, atmosphere polluted. Nothing is left to hand on, so there is no transfer that could release it.

The permanence is real but not as punishing as it first sounds, because of [[retroactive-reweighting]]: what a past tonne of CO₂ *costs* is the current cost of undoing it. Get better at capture and every historical emission in the world weighs less.

So: **the event is immutable, the weight is live.**

## Why it works this way

If consumption debit could be discharged by transfer, everyone would hand their burned fuel to a shell entity and the accounting would be a fiction within a year. Permanence is what makes [[no-externalities]] enforceable.

Making the *weight* float rather than the event is what keeps permanence from being merely punitive. It also aligns the incentive correctly: you cannot undo your emission, but you can fund the science that makes it cheaper to undo — which lowers your own past burden and everyone else's simultaneously. That is unusually good incentive design and worth highlighting in public writing.
