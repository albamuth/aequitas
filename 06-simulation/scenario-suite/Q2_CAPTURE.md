# Captured & Wasted Labour-Time — Q2 (plain-language companion)

> Companion to [`q2_capture.py`](q2_capture.py). Fourth sim of the [scenario suite](scenario_suite_METHOD.md).
> **Question:** what is the impact of exploitation, cronyism, capitalism, and war on stolen labour-time?

## First, the reframe (it matters)

**Under Aequitas there is nothing to steal.** Credit is non-transferable (A3) — it's a fact about who did the work, and facts about people don't change hands. So "stolen labour-time" isn't an Aequitas quantity at all. The answerable question is: **how big is the pool of hours that today gets *captured* by owners or *wasted* on enforcement — a pool that Aequitas makes structurally impossible or lets wither?**

## The answer

**Roughly a fifth to well over a third of all productive labour-time — about 185–396 hours per adult per year — is currently either captured by ownership or spent enforcing/extracting. Aequitas dissolves the capture outright (A3) and shrinks the enforcement (no rent to guard, no profit to skim, far less inequality to police).**

Two *distinct* pools (they answer different questions and overlap, so they're never naively summed):

**Pool A — Capture (distributional).** Capital takes ~31% of US national income (BEA); the genuinely *extractive* part (excluding imputed home-owner "rent" and the labour half of proprietors' income) is ~55–85% of that → **17–26% of labour, ~185–286 h/adult/yr**. This is the product of your work that accrues to someone for *owning*, not working. **A3 dissolves it** — with non-transferable credit there's no rentier income to receive.

**Pool B — Wasteful allocation (guard labour).** Bowles & Jayadev's "guard labour" — supervisors, guards, police, prisons, military — is ~20–29% of the US workforce and *rising with inequality*. The part that shrinks under Aequitas (leaving genuine public safety and real coordination) is ~40–70% of it → **8–20% of labour, ~87–220 h/adult/yr**. War/military (~2.8% of the workforce, ~30 h/adult) is a *subset* reported inside this, never added on top.

**Combined, de-duplicated: 17–36% of productive labour.** A supervisor is both captured-from and guard-labour, so the pools overlap; the honest union runs from the larger single pool up to a partial overlap — well below the naive 47% sum.

## Why this is the deep link to the rest of the theory

- The capture pool is the quantitative face of §1 (inequality as a machine): the loop where owning lets you claim others' work. A3 breaks the loop.
- The guard-labour pool is *self-reinforcing under money*: inequality requires enforcement, enforcement is a cost, the cost falls on the producers. Aequitas removes the thing being guarded.
- **This freed pool is the input to Q5** — the hours that could instead go to healthcare, housing, and renewables.

## Honesty ledger

- **Every fraction here is contestable and shown as a band.** The extractive fraction of capital income and the shrinkable fraction of guard labour are judgment calls, not measurements — flagged as such.
- **Not all capture is "cronyism," and not all guard labour is waste.** Some capital income funds real risk-bearing; some supervision is genuine coordination; some policing addresses real harm. The bands are deliberately wide to hold that honesty.
- **These are US, snapshot, income-accounting estimates** — an order-of-magnitude sizing, not a precise figure. The *structural* claim (A3 dissolves capture; enforcement withers) is the robust part.
- **"Freed" is potential, not automatic.** Reallocating those hours needs the demand side (pledges) to pull them elsewhere — that's Q5, and it's an upper envelope.
