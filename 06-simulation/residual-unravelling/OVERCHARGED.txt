==============================================================================
WHAT THIS FOUND
==============================================================================

  1. THE CLASS IS REAL AND IT IS SMALL. At most 2.50% of the
     population is over-charged AND unable to afford proof, and that
     is at the first round, before anyone has moved.

  2. IT IS BOUNDED BY THE DISCLOSURE COST, NOT BY THE ESTIMATE.
     Anyone over-charged by MORE than their cost of proving it
     discloses. Only the thin band over-charged by LESS than that
     stays, so the mean over-charge on the trapped is 0.03 debit
     units against estimates of 1.00 to 7.34 -- about 1 to 3%.

  3. THE CONTROL IS THE RESULT. Under the rule Foundations 4.4
     REJECTS -- estimating over the whole population -- the estimate
     never moves, so THE TRAPPED CLASS NEVER CLEARS. 50 agents stay
     over-charged forever and 1,050 stay dark. Under the residual
     rule both go to zero.

  So the answer to @custos: the class exists, the mean cannot see it,
  it is bounded by disclosure cost rather than by the estimate, and
  the residual rule liquidates it while the rejected rule preserves
  it permanently.
