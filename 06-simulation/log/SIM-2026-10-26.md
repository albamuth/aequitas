With joiners rebuilt to birth (floor credit, 1,380 h/yr of past debit, mean age 39.8, everyone joining on day one, 100 yearly periods), **ρ\*_L is 0.416 at F = 10, against 1.206 one-day: a fall of 66%. 2. **F = 2.** Open defect 2 in the stable-band README (`essential` flag unset) refuses negative-room joiners in full, essentials included. Not fixed, because the brief keeps me to new code. F = 2 is a lower bound on a corrected run; F = 10 and 14 sit just above their own negative-room thresholds (ρ 0.378 and 0.270).
3. **A steady state.** No deaths, births or later joiners, and κ and `R_max` fixed for 100 years. The 150-year return is a model number.
4. **Anything about a growing network.** Everyone joins on day one, which is the largest one-time windfall.
5. Past credit is floor only and past debit is flat for everyone. Population seed and the illustrative CAP = 0.85 were not varied.
