## What this does not show
1. **"No upper edge" comes from the capacity input.** At capacity 0.85 x wants with the German kappa the one-day edge is 0.651 / 0.211 / 0.162, and the German broad basket at F = 2 (lower edge up to 0.846) would be closed. The opening depends on the Q6 capacity figures and on nothing else here.
2. **Baskets are scaled, not measured (Assumption A).** Essentials may not shrink by the whole-lifestyle ratio.
3. **Open does not mean the gate is idle.** At F = 2 German, rho_sat is 2.68, so a tolerance below that still holds some people below their wants.
4. The population's demand spread (log-normal, sigma 0.45) is invented and shared with earlier runs; the joiner's past debit is set equal to the method's own yearly figure; everyone joins on day one; no pledge term.
5. The 883 and 759 figures are quoted from Foundations 5.5.3. They do not appear as those digits in `median-lifestyle/Q6.md`; the ratios agree with the `EFF` constants to 3 decimals.
