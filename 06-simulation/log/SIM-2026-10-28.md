# Simulation night — 2026-10-28

## Verdict
The "6.25x" holds only for one product on one dimension: the steer's hide, energy only (12.50 vs 2.00 MJ/kg). In collapsed debit-hours per kg the same gap is **4.10x** (0.5437 vs 0.1327 h/kg, illustrative weights), the other steer products sit at 2.5x to 2.9x, and the real DOE refinery's coarse-over-true runs 1.25x to 2.56x. How often an absent step is never-looked-at rather than recorded-as-absent **cannot be answered from this project's data**: it is the sheet-maker's enumeration rate q, no real flow-sheet corpus is on disk, and the model sweeps it. At q = 0.5 a reader with no scope line is wrong by about **50% of the true figure on both chains** (steer 50.1 ± 14.7%, 0.0794 ± 0.0252 h/kg; refinery 49.3 ± 12.5%) if they read absent as not-passed, and by 162% (steer) or 104% (refinery) if they read it as maybe-passed. The pre-declared hope that a scope line fixes this **failed**: a reader who charges every never-looked-at step was off by 80.9% (steer) and 51.9% (refinery), worse than trusting. A scope line wins only against a producer who chooses what to leave off, where trusting understates every product by up to 100% and the scoped reader understates none. Nothing here moves a number in the documents; it bears on how the passage claim is worded.

## What this does not show
1. **How often real plants leave steps never-looked-at.** q is swept, not measured. No real flow-sheet corpus is in `06-simulation/data/`.
2. **That these chains are typical.** The steer's digits are illustrative, and the refinery's passage table is a declared standard configuration. 28 and 56 cells.
3. **A refinery figure in hours.** Its energy and masses are on different scales, so only ratios between readers hold there. The steer's hours use the unsourced 0.002 h/MJ weight.
4. **That the scope line is worthless.** It stopped the producer-chosen omission and cost accuracy on honest sheets. Its price and the checking of an "enumerated-and-absent" record are not modelled.
5. **Clustering.** Cells are treated as independent. Real unlisted steps cluster by unit.
