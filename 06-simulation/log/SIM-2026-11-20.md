# Simulation night — 2026-11-20

## Verdict
Seller choice does not reach a generous span estimate by the same route it reaches a floor. A floor is published, and a control run showed sellers arrest it (a F = 14 network drops from 82% of joiners to 0.02%). A span is not published, so the arrest works only if sellers read the public log for backdated credit, which is my assumption and not something the documents state. With that reading, 200 sampled accounts catch inflation of about 2 years or more, and the network falls from a 20% share to near 0 at 3 years or more. Without it, informed joiners move 34% at +1 year and 91% at +5 years. The gap below 2 years stays open: at +1 year a network is flagged in 7% of rounds and keeps 32% of informed joiners, 12 points over the 20% baseline. The recruiting lever is pledge budget, not consumption: inside the rationing band each extra year lowers the joiner's gate slack by 87 to 638 h.
