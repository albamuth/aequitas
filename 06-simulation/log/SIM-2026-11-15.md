## What this does not show
1. **It does not show food sits in E.** Essentials-first gives "no rho can touch the line"; a narrower E gives 0.192. Opposite answers, and a network chooses.
2. **Line wants are assumed proportional to overall wants.** The 31.7% depends on that. A flatter distribution for fruit would raise it. Not run.
3. **The aggregate adds units across lines.** A surplus of other goods cannot become fruit, so "none below 4.0" says the gate has nothing to ration overall and says nothing about the 49% hole in the one line.
4. **The line is fresh produce only.** Processed fruit and vegetables cannot be separated in EXIOBASE, so g is understated.
5. No substitution to other foods, one year only, US line share scaled by 0.836 and not measured for Germany.
