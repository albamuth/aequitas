# Simulation night — 2026-10-18

## Verdict
When the flow sheet cannot resolve parcel identity, the books overstate in proportion to the failure rate and stay below the naive sum. On the real refinery chain, for a buyer holding all 7 co-products, the overstatement is 1.13× at an unresolved rate `u` of 0.05, 1.26× at 0.10, 1.53× at 0.20 and 2.33× at 0.50 (naive sum: 3.63×; the worst of 4,000 trials at 0.50 was 3.53×). **The sharper finding is on the other policy.** Refusing to publish (`not identified`) never touches a single co-product's per-kg figure, which needs no identity. It refuses 15% of two-co-product holdings and 77% of the full holding at `u` = 0.10, and half of the full holding by `u` = 0.05. How often real sheets fail is not answered: `u` is swept, and no plant data exists here. Nothing in the documents moves. Six of seven declared cells held. One failed: the twin crossover is at 0.58, outside the declared 0.15 to 0.45.

## What this does not show
- **How often real flow sheets fail.** `u` is a swept parameter. There is no plant data in the project.
- **Whether failures come in clusters.** Links fail independently here. A lost mix record would lose a whole tank's links at once, so the P3 shares are upper estimates for one document failing.
- **Whether row 7 catches the gap.** The run treats row 10e's remedy as the interval [P2, P1]. Whether row 7 in practice finds a missing mix event is not simulated.
- **Real twins.** No twin parcels exist in the DOE data. Their quantities were resampled from the refinery's own steps.
- **Other dimensions.** Only energy is run. Labour and pollutants are not.
