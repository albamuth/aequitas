## What this does not show
1. **That CAP is 0.85.** It is an illustrative input. The answer is 100% below about 0.87 and 78% at 0.95. At CAP ≥ 1.0 the gate never rations.
2. **That a filer can realise the saving.** The run measures the incentive. It does not model the chance or cost of the deletion being contradicted by evidence.
3. **A limit on years deleted.** The gain is linear in years. Nothing in the arithmetic stops at a filer's real age.
4. **That d is the filer's true consumption in the deleted years.** It was swept at five values. A filer with half the median (690 h) gains nothing.
5. **A pledge term.** P was held at 0. A pledge-doubled effective ρ (4.6) would push more cells above break-even and was not run.
