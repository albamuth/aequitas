## What this does not show
1. It is not the requested energy table. The shares are value shares of fossil purchases, not energy content.
2. The cut x share rule assumes every other input substitutes for the lost fuel. Fixed proportions (output falls with fuel) was not run and is the usual picture of a blockade.
3. The per-line against even comparison is entailed by pooled versus separate capacity; it says nothing about fuel.
4. The product-line mapping (which EXIOBASE products are "food", "utilities", "non-essentials", with motor fuel inside non-essentials) is mine; one population draw (seed 7); imports and domestic fuel are not told apart.
