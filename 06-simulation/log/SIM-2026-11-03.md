# Simulation night — 2026-11-03

## Verdict
In a network that adopts over 20 years with no change to who joins, **78% of all days lived are attested at year 20, falling to 42% at year 40 and 17% at year 60** (absolute: 206,030 members at year 20). The person-level picture is far wider than the average: at year 20 a tenth of members sit at 0% (born inside) and a tenth above 95%. A single cohort falls as a ÷ (a + s): people who join at 30–49 are at 0.80 after 10 years, 0.66 after 20 and 0.48 after 40. **The ceiling's bound is therefore an instrument on at most about a fifth of days in the first twenty years, and on most days only after about year 40.** Every figure depends on an assumed joining-age mix; no adoption data exists.

3. It does not show whether the bound works on the observed span. It sizes the span.
4. Children joining at birth is an assumption; the network's consent rule (§4.6) could change it.
