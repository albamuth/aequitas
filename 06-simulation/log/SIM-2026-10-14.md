## What this does not show
1. **O1 is a proof restated as a search.** A search cannot refute a true theorem; its value is catching an evaluator that mis-states a premise, which it did in run 1. It says nothing about any real network's implementation.
2. **A daily-rate ledger, not an event log.** Records are day-blocks at a constant rate. The project's IC-7 runs a sliding 24-hour window over timed roles; this run's sweep is its calendar-day form and was not run against the audits schema or sub-day placement.
3. **Four premises is what this run could see, not a proof that the list is complete.** Run 1 found one the first draft had not listed.
4. **The pledge budget is assumed equal to earned credit** (condition 5). Room `P` is out of scope for *claimed credit* and is not modelled.
5. **The DST figure is the size of a stand-in**, one 25-hour day a year, not a measurement of any calendar implementation.
6. **Whether the bound matters is not asked here.** The ceiling-rubric finding that the statistic reads no accounts is unaffected either way.

## Does it touch the documents?
**§5.5.5, the five conditions.** They name `F` as convention (P1), fraud under IC-7 (P2, without saying the cap is evaluated on the account-day sum across all records including backdated filings), one network's books, and the budget rule. **They do not name P3 or P4**: that days lived is the span of §4.4 condition 3 — the earliest date the evidence permits, with the floor credited over all of it — nor that the denominator is elapsed time over human subscribers. The construction that needs no cap breach of any kind (O3) is stopped only by §4.4. A sixth condition or a cross-reference from §5.5.5 to §4.4 condition 3 is the candidate fold. **Not edited.**
**SB-01 §3 and §8.** The brief defines the statistic as `max ÷ min` and writes self-tests 1–2 over `max ÷ F`. They are different objects and attack (c) reaches only the first. The brief should say which it means. Not edited; it is inside `06-simulation/` but it is the author's published brief.
**Conformance row 8** already entails the account-day reading (*"no account claims more than 24 hours of activity in 24 hours"*), and `audits/arithmetic_audits.py` implements it that way. Nothing moves there.
