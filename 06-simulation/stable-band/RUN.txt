  Terms, before any number:
    F      the FLOOR, hours a day credited for staying alive. A dial.
    rho    the DEBIT TOLERANCE in the gate D <= rho*C. A dial.
    E      hours of other people's labour a YEAR OF ESSENTIALS commands.
           Exogenous. Nobody has measured it, so it is swept.
    R_max  what the economy can physically deliver. Exogenous.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE PRODUCTION METHOD DOES TO THE BAND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The physical envelope B is FIXED -- same energy, same materials.
  What changes is the debit each unit of the SAME real standard costs.
  Source: 06-simulation/median-lifestyle/Q6.md (EXIOBASE, measured).

  NOTE ON THE ANCHOR. 'One median lifestyle' = 1,380 h/yr = one median
  AMERICAN lifestyle. Q6 measured the US as the inefficient outlier:
  Germany, Japan and Spain reach a comparable-or-better standard, and
  longer lives, on about two thirds of the embodied labour.

                    method  debit/unit  real capacity  rho*(F=10)  % held back
                 US method       13.80         56,446        1.20        35.8%
 German or Japanese method        8.83         88,196        4.00         0.0%  NEVER BINDS (rho grid ends here)
            Spanish method        7.59        102,629        4.00         0.0%  NEVER BINDS (rho grid ends here)

  Unconstrained wants total 66,407 real units.

  READ THE CAPACITY COLUMN AGAINST THAT NUMBER, and the result is not
  the one this table was built expecting.

  Under the US method the same physical envelope delivers LESS than
  people want, so the gate rations and about a third are held back.
  Under the German, Japanese or Spanish method the SAME envelope
  delivers MORE than everyone wants -- so the gate never binds at any
  rho in the swept range, and nobody is held back at all.

  In plain words: the band has an upper edge only because the US
  method is wasteful. Fix the method and there is nothing left to
  ration. Foundations Sec.5.5.3 calls that the intended end state,
  not a failure -- 'where the economy can actually deliver that much,
  this is abundance'.

  What one median AMERICAN lifestyle costs, by the method used to make it:

                    method     h/yr of other labour   vs the US method
                 US method                    1,380              100%
 German or Japanese method                      883               64%
            Spanish method                      759               55%

  The ledger charges the method, not the person. Two people living the
  same material life carry different debit if one is supplied by a
  wasteful chain -- which is A4 and A5 doing their work with no
  mandate, no ban, and nobody's consumption forbidden.

==============================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS, in plain words
==============================================================================
  Each row is one choice of floor. The two rho columns are the lowest
  and highest debit tolerance that BOTH keep essentials affordable AND
  leave the ledger still rationing. A width of 0 means no rho works at
  that floor, and the network fails whatever it sets.

  '% held back' is the share of people the gate holds below what they
  wanted, at the low and high edge of the band. It is a description,
  not a test -- no threshold on it is used anywhere above.
