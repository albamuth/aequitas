## Why this matters to Aequitas

Proudhon is Aequitas's direct ancestor on the *diagnosis*: he named rent, interest, and profit as **extraction** — income taken without doing work — rather than fair payment. That connects to Aequitas's rules that a price is just a cost with no profit ([Foundations §A5](../docs/Aequitas_Foundations_v0.16.md#a5-price--cost)), that holding property is a burden rather than an income engine ([Foundations §3.2](../docs/Aequitas_Foundations_v0.16.md#32-the-two-kinds-of-debit--and-the-two-components-of-property-debit)), and that capitalism can't operate inside the system ([Foundations §7.1](../docs/Aequitas_Foundations_v0.16.md#71-capitalism-cannot-function)).

He matters just as much as a **cautionary tale**: his *remedy* was a new kind of money, and it failed the way money-based remedies fail.

## Key findings

- **"Property is theft"** (1840) — but with a distinction that later Marxists dropped. Proudhon split **property** (holdings that earn you an income off *other people's* labour — a rented building, a loan at interest) from **possession** (the things you actually use and live in). Only the first is "theft." He did *not* reject people having and using their own things.
- **A just price is the labour a thing took to make** — you trade at cost, not at whatever the buyer can be squeezed for.
- **Mutual credit:** people extend credit to one another **at no interest**, cutting out state money and banks, and with them the fees that financiers skim.
- Capital, he said, is **"dead labour"** — work already done and paid for, producing nothing further on its own. Rent and interest are therefore income for no labour.
- His attempt to build it, the **People's Bank** (Paris, 1849), collapsed within months; Proudhon was jailed for press offences around the same time.
- Josiah Warren reached the same core doctrine independently, and earlier, in an actual shop — see [[cost-the-limit-of-price]].

## What we can use

- **The possession/property split is a direct forerunner of Aequitas's treatment of what you hold.** In Aequitas you hold and use things freely, but holding them puts a *burden* on your books, not an income — you carry a thing's material weight while it's yours, and it goes with the thing when you pass it on. This is the clean answer to the fear that Aequitas confiscates people's belongings: it doesn't. It just stops belongings from being an earning machine.
- **"Capital is dead labour"** is a tight, quotable way of saying why Aequitas can't have lending-at-interest: there's nothing productive to rent out, because a record of past work can't be handed to anyone (see [[non-fungibility]]).
- It gives the anti-rent position a **186-year pedigree.** Aequitas isn't a novelty — it's an old diagnosis with a genuinely new mechanism.
- Proudhon and Warren arriving at the same rule *independently* is a fair argument that pricing-at-cost is something people keep *discovering*, not an arbitrary invention.

## What it gets wrong / limitations

- **His cure was a currency.** Mutual credit can be passed around, lent, and piled up — so it can be concentrated, and the very inequality Proudhon diagnosed grows back inside his own solution. Aequitas's answer is its hardest break with the whole mutualist tradition: **credit is a record of who did the work and can never be transferred** ([Foundations §A3 (non-fungibility)](../docs/Aequitas_Foundations_v0.16.md#a3-non-fungibility)). **This is the key point to make whenever someone asks "isn't this just mutualism?"**
- **Pricing by labour-time drags back the old skill/effort problem** — the same one that defeated Warren (is a surgeon's hour worth a ditch-digger's hour?). Aequitas escapes it by counting *time spent*, not effort, and resolving skill and danger as physical costs — see [Foundations §A2 (time as measure)](../docs/Aequitas_Foundations_v0.16.md#a2-time-as-measure).
- **No account of pollution or ecological cost.** A pure labour theory has nowhere to put the runoff and the smoke. Aequitas's "every consequence is on the books" principle has no Proudhonian ancestor — that idea comes from the calculation-in-kind tradition instead (see [Neurath: calculation in kind](../docs/GLOSSARY.md#src-neurath-calculation-in-kind)).
- The People's Bank failed on the practical mechanics of clearing and scale, not on principle. **Follow up: get the actual post-mortem** — it likely predicts the same failure modes for any settlement design Aequitas might use.
- Proudhon held well-documented antisemitic and misogynist views. **Cite the economics; do not lionise the man.** Expect this to come up in any public writing that names him, and get ahead of it.
