## Verdict in one line
"One of the most carefully constructed alternative economic proposals I have encountered." Strengths methodological (objections register, adversarial stress-testing, versioning), conceptual (physical-trace test, front-loading, non-fungibility), technical (recursion convergence). Weaknesses: **accessibility** (jargon density) and **deferred architectural risk** (trust networks / OP-22 / OP-24 load-bearing but underspecified).

## What the reviewer says works
- **Cost/value distinction (§0)** — sharpest entry point; inoculates against the supply-side critique.
- **Physical-trace test** — transferable, teachable; cleanly separates OP-17 / OP-18 / OP-23.
- **Overview problem/solution structure**, sandwich lifecycle, roof-repair identity — concrete and memorable.
- **Objections register format** — exemplary; signals intellectual honesty.
- **Sraffa/Steedman reply (B7)** — strongest technical result; "show, don't argue."
- **Debit-follows-possession (B10)**, **front-loading (B3)**, **objective-function screen** — all praised.

## Friction points (accessibility)
1. **Abbreviation density** — OP-1…25, C1…12, A1…8, IC-1…12, P4/P8/P9, §x.x refs in the first pages. No master glossary/index.
2. "One-sentence theory" is a paragraph — relabel "the core claim."
3. In-text versioning ("new in v0.5") clutters for evaluators — move to a changes appendix.
4. **Debit taxonomy (§3.2) is load-bearing but text-only** — wants a visual schematic.

## Argument-level concerns
- **OP-16 (onerousness)** underweight — should be 🔴. 45y of time-banking shows chronic skill shortage under flat-hour crediting. All four proposed fixes speculative; (a) hour-ceiling reintroduces a rating mechanism (who sets ceilings?). **Honest admission owed: A2 may carry a structural cost in labour allocation.**
- **OP-24 (understatement drift)** relies on rival-sector audit — unproven; breaks down for sectors with no natural rival (water treatment, care work, CO₂ baseline). §C Test 2 sim is load-bearing.
- **Disparity ceiling (§7.5) more fragile than presented.** Depends on: (1) self-care floor in a narrow band, (2) counterparty re-computation arresting floor-shopping (OP-14), (3) OP-22 solved, (4) no fraud manufacturing gross hours (OP-1). **Presented as arithmetic certainty; it is a conditional result — if OP-22 solved, then the ceiling holds.**
- **A4 "no externalities" is aspirational** — OP-24 erodes it without breaking an equation. Tension between axiomatic confidence and empirical drift.
- **Trust networks (Level 2) under-theorized** relative to load — verification, weighting governance, always-creditable lists, audit, dispute resolution all route through them, yet C2 is deferred. Large deferred liability. OP-10/22/24/A10 all route here.
- **Self-care proof-of-life verification is weak** — if self-care credit → full pledging power, phantom-human incentive (OP-15) becomes severe.
