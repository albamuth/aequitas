## 0. The headline finding

**Aequitas's hard problem is division, not measurement.**

Every predecessor that tried to account in physical units died at the same place, and it was never the measuring. Totals are physically well-defined: a refinery consumed *X* joules and emitted *Y* tonnes; a team of nine built the bridge. **The splits are not.** How much of the refinery's debit belongs to the diesel versus the naphtha? How much of the bridge belongs to the welder versus the surveyor? Physics returns no answer, because "belongs to" is not a physical relation.

**No amount of better instrumentation resolves a co-product split, because the indeterminacy is not epistemic.** Refining forever converges on nothing.

*Foundations v0.3 accepted this:* §3.4 now separates resolution (improves with instruments) from allocation (does not), and §1.1 declares the three quantities that are conventions rather than measurements. **The diagnosis is adopted; the two hard cases remain open.**

The thesis was confirmed four times in one session — co-products (OP-17), team credit (OP-18), credit categories, and audience division (OP-21) — and the two that closed did so by **dissolving the division**, not by finding the right split. **When a division looks unanswerable, first ask whether the question is malformed.**

## A1 — OP-17. Joint production has no physical allocation

**Attacks:** A1, A5, and the **universality criterion** directly.
**Sources:** [Sraffa/Steedman/Morishima on negative labour values](https://www.scienceopen.com/hosted-document?doi=10.13169/worlrevipoliecon.14.1.0063) · [ISO 14044 allocation critique](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11367-016-1161-2) · `02-research/joint-production-allocation-problem.md`
**Status:** 🔴 **Open. The most dangerous item in the project, and it blocks C3.**

A process yields beef and leather, or mutton and wool, or a refinery's full fraction slate. One physical event, several outputs, one pool of debit. Splitting by mass, energy content, exergy, or economic value gives **four different answers, none more physically true than the others.** ISO 14044 ranks the options and then, when the physical ones are "inappropriate," falls back to **market price** — which A5 forbids, since price is supposed to be the *output* of the accounting, not an input.

**This is not an EEIO artifact.** EEIO allocates by price *because there is nothing else to allocate by*. Swapping data source does not escape it.

The classical form is sharper: under joint production, labour values can go **negative** — a commodity "containing" less than zero labour. Any single-substance cost accounting inherits this, and Aequitas is a single-substance cost accounting.

**Candidates:**
1. **Refuse to split.** Carry debit as an irreducible *set* on the joint output bundle, resolved only at consumption by what the consumer actually took. Preserves universality; explodes C1's parcel model.
2. **System expansion / avoided burden.** ISO's preferred escape: credit the co-product with the debit of what it displaces. Needs a counterfactual — a judgement, not a measurement. **Currently the most promising.**
3. **Declare a rule as core protocol** and accept it is a convention. Now survivable, because Foundations §1.1 exists to hold declared conventions — a declared convention is not an ad-hoc rule; a disguised one is.

**Disqualifying test:** run one rule over a slaughterhouse, an oil refinery, and a CHP plant. If it needs a different justification in each, it is wrong.

## A2 — OP-10 / P8. Weighting-model governance, and the Hayek residue

**Attacks:** A8, decentralization.
**Status:** 🔴 Open. Largest hole in A8.

Whoever sets the mitigation-cost model controls every balance in history without touching a core rule.

**The comparison sharpens it.** Parecon's Iteration Facilitation Board is [attacked as implausible](https://ejpe.org/journal/article/view/867) for assuming a body can announce opportunity costs for all goods, resources, labour categories and capital stocks. **Aequitas's weighting-model maintainer is structurally the same object.** The verification ladder answers *data collection* — it gathers locally rather than centrally — but it does not answer *model maintenance*, which remains central by default.

The strongest available reply is competing local variance under A8: multiple weighting models, openly published, each recomputable by anyone from the same log. **That reply is asserted in A8 and nowhere specified. Specify it.**

**Entangled with OP-9** — a scarcity price is the dual of an optimisation, so whoever sets the objective function sets every scarcity price. And with Foundations §10.1: a trust network that can declare an activity creditable can issue credit. **Three problems, one capture surface. Work them together.**

## A3 — OP-18. Responsibility for joint work is not divisible

**Attacks:** A1's attribution claim, A2, C1's agent field.
**Sources:** [Ellerman](http://et.worldeconomicsassociation.org/files/WEA-ET-5-1-Ellerman.pdf) · `02-research/ellerman-labor-theory-of-property.md`
**Status:** 🔴 Open. Same shape as OP-17, one level up.

Ellerman is explicit that de facto responsibility for a joint product is **joint and non-decomposable**. The whole team is responsible for the whole output. There is no factual measure saying the welder caused 40% of the bridge — and Aequitas needs exactly that number to credit nine people separately.

**Hours-worked is an allocation convention, not a measurement** — the same category error as splitting a refinery by mass. It is defensible and probably right, but it is a choice.

*Progress:* Foundations §1.1 now declares it as a convention rather than presenting it as a physical fact. **That converts a universality violation into an honest one. It does not make the convention correct**, and a critic can still ask why hours rather than some other basis.

*(The ally half of Ellerman — responsibility imputation as the grounding for A1 — is adopted and lives in Part B, B6.)*

## A4 — OP-16. The onerousness gap

**Attacks:** A2, and the system's ability to allocate labour at all.
**Sources:** [Hahnel](https://znetwork.org/znetarticle/in-defense-of-participatory-economics-by-robin-hahnel/) · [PLOS One time-banking review, 2025](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0322760) · `02-research/participatory-economics.md`
**Status:** 🟠 **Half answered.** The remaining half has no good candidate.

Parecon remunerates **duration, intensity, and onerousness**. Aequitas refuses to rate-scale labour (A2) and resolves differences materially instead.

**What A2 v0.3 now covers:** exertion (extra calories), hazard (retroactive health debit), and — new — **skill**, since training is credited work rather than a downstream charge. That last one matters: the old rule made the *service* expensive without ever rewarding the *trainee*, answering a pricing question and leaving the incentive question open.

**What remains uncovered: disutility with no material signature.** Tedium, isolation, indignity. Two jobs with identical calories, identical training, and identical health outcomes credit identically per hour — but nobody wants one of them.

**This is not theoretical.** Time banking credits every hour identically — exactly A2's flat hour — and 45 years across dozens of countries produces chronic **skill mismatch**, credit hoarding, and skills in short supply. Warren's 1830 failure is dismissible as one man in one storefront; this is a peer-reviewed literature.

**Aequitas's counter to Parecon stays valid:** effort ratings are criticised as unmeasurable and as requiring pervasive surveillance and humiliation, and material resolution avoids that entirely. But avoiding a bad answer is not having a good one.

**Candidates:**
- **(a) Hour-ceiling differentiation — currently strongest.** Pay the premium in *hours, not rate*: a sustainable sewer shift is 4h, a pleasant one 8h; both credit 1hr/hr, so no multiplier exists anywhere. Survives A2 because the justification is **physiological and measured** — occupational limits on night work, heat, and repetitive strain are already regulated. Weakness: who sets the ceilings → **OP-10**, and self-reported onerousness needs an evidentiary basis.
- **(b) Check how much of OP-16 is simply unmeasured hazard.** Night shift, isolation, and repetitive strain have documented health costs that A2's hazard clause **already** injects. **Do this before designing anything** — it may shrink the problem substantially.
- **(c) Automation pressure.** Unfilled pledges are a visible, quantified signal saying *automate this*. Doesn't staff the sewer tonight; funds the machine that ends the job.
- **(d) Rotation** (balanced job complexes). Works, carries [the compulsory-labour critique](http://libcom.org/blog/workers-critique-parecon-11042012). Last resort.

> **Withdrawn candidate:** *"route onerous work to service credit."* This was the favoured fix until it was established that production, service and enrichment are **one credit (time)**, not separate types. There is no separate currency to pay a premium in. Recorded so it is not re-proposed.

**Test:** compute the debit-cost of night-shift office cleaner, day-shift office cleaner, and rural postal carrier under the current model. **If the numbers come out identical, OP-16 is confirmed and (b) is eliminated.** Cheap once C3 exists, and it decides between candidates rather than arguing about them.

## A5 — OP-6. Feedback mechanics

**Attacks:** §6.3, §6.4. **Status:** 🟠 Open, and **promoted**.

How signals aggregate without becoming a popularity plutocracy.

**Why it is more urgent than its old ranking implied.** Accumulation is forbidden by A3, and a high producer's marginal credit does nothing once their ceiling exceeds their appetite. **Feedback and pledging are therefore the entire motivation system for anyone past their own consumption ceiling** — which is to say, for exactly the people the economy most depends on.

**Two live sub-problems:**
1. **Signal flooding** *(inherited from OP-20)*. Signals are unbacked and plentiful by design, and generation cost for low-quality content trends to zero. Nothing in the schema constrains this; it is a projection-side problem and it has no answer yet.
2. **OP-8 reframed — can feedback be bought?** The old firewall question dissolved once feedback stopped being a credit type. What survives is sharper: **a signal that credit can purchase is a currency by the back door.**

## A6 — P4. Abolishing property income does not abolish class

**Attacks:** the "surgical, keep functional institutions" positioning in §8; constrains OP-1.
**Sources:** [Albert & Hahnel on the coordinator class](https://znetwork.org/znetarticle/parecon-and-anarcho-syndicalism-an-interview-with-michael-albert-by-michael-albert/) · `02-research/participatory-economics.md`
**Status:** 🟠 **Weakened but alive.** *Substantially rewritten in v0.4 — the original form of this objection was based on a misreading.*

Parecon's central historical claim: Soviet-type systems abolished capitalists and produced a new ruling class anyway — a **coordinator class** monopolising empowering work.

> **⚠️ What v0.3 got wrong.** The register previously argued that *"OP-1 converts service credit into influence,"* producing a flywheel where empowering roles accumulate service credit, which accumulates governance weight, which protects the roles. **That argument is void.** There is no separate service credit: production, service and enrichment are **one credit, measured in time** (Foundations §6), and credit tracks **hours, not material throughput** (A2). A carer's hour and a steelworker's hour confer identical weight. **No credit-accumulation flywheel exists.**

**What survives, and it is a real objection.** The coordinator-class risk was never *only* about income — that is the whole point of Parecon's argument. It is about **who holds empowering work**, and Aequitas remains exposed for one structural reason: **§8 deliberately keeps existing institutions** — municipal government, planning bodies, civil service. Those are precisely the institutions in which conceptual and agenda-setting work is concentrated. Removing profit does not redistribute *that*.

**The new residual, introduced by pledging.** Pledging power accrues per hour worked, equally for everyone. That is egalitarian across *sectors* — the fix for the withdrawn version — but it weights by **hours available to work**, which is not equally distributed. Caregivers, part-time workers, the disabled, and the chronically ill hold systematically less say in what gets produced. **This is a live and unaddressed distributional problem**, and it is the sharpest form P4 now takes.

**Consequences for OP-1:** it cannot be designed without answering *who games this?*, and the answer is **whoever already holds an empowering institutional role**. Of the candidates, *proposal power with universal suffrage* is the only one that structurally separates agenda-setting from deciding. Parecon's own rule — **decision weight proportional to how much you are affected** — is a fifth candidate, and it does not accumulate at all, which makes it the best available answer to the hours-inequality residual.

## A7 — OP-9 / P5. Preference revelation

**Attacks:** A5. The Mises/Hayek line of attack.
**Sources:** `02-research/neurath-calculation-in-kind.md` · [Dapprich](https://brightagebeyond.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/dapprich-philipp-2021-optimal-planning-with-consumer-feedback-a-simulation-of-a-socialist-economy.pdf) · `02-research/kantorovich-shadow-prices.md` · `02-research/cockshott-cottrell-labour-time.md`
**Status:** 🟠 **Substantially answered. Needs writing up, not inventing.**

Cost says what a thing takes. It does not rank two people who both want the last one. Under A5 the price of a unique lakeside house equals its material cost — absurd, since demand is unbounded at that price and nothing rations it.

**Two answers now exist, and they are complementary:**

1. **Pledges supply the demand signal** (Foundations §6.4). Cost says what a thing takes; pledges say who wants it — generated with no prices, no central optimizer, and **no Iteration Facilitation Board.** This is the decentralized answer Parecon needed a standing body to fake, and it is the project's strongest reply to Hayek on this axis.
2. **Scarcity-as-debit handles rationing.** On [Kantorovich's](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1975/kantorovich/lecture/) objectively determined valuations, a shadow price is the **cost of a binding constraint**, not a margin extracted by a seller. Taking the last unit of a constrained resource imposes a real cost on everyone else — the next-best substitute, or relieving the constraint. **Recorded as debit rather than skimmed as margin, this is compatible with A5 and arguably required by A4.** A5 forbids profit in exchange; it does not say opportunity cost is not a cost.

**P5 — the Cockshott variant, and why it also closes here.** Cockshott & Cottrell's demand lever is the *gap* between market-clearing price and labour value; production expands or contracts as that gap moves. **A5 collapses that gap to zero by construction**, so Aequitas inherits their problem with one fewer instrument. Their critics' line — *calculation in labour values does not factor in demand, and supply without demand is blind* — lands harder on Aequitas than on them. **Pledges are the replacement instrument.** Same wound, same cure.

**Remaining catch:** a dual price requires a primal optimisation, which requires an objective function, which is a social judgement — **straight into OP-10.** Federating it per-constraint may help, since most scarcity constraints are local (this lake, this ore body) rather than global. **Unverified; worth a design pass.**

## A8 — OP-22. Minimum audit disclosure

**Attacks:** §5.3. **Status:** 🔽 **Narrowed from a foundational conflict to an implementation question.**

The original objection — that meme tracing needs a durable provenance trail while the privacy layer is designed to destroy one — was overstated on two counts. The correct bar for attribution is **no worse than today** (a gallery buyer already has no proof the artist painted the work), and provenance only becomes fraught in the capitalized art market that A5 removes. On privacy, the world already runs counterparty-visible and third-party-opaque, and Aequitas replaces neither courts nor social pressure.

**What survives is narrow and technical.** Banking externalizes validation to institutions; **Aequitas has no institution to externalize to.** Verification is the Level 2 trust ecosystem, and an auditor must be able to see *something*. So: **what is the minimum an auditor must see to verify a claim without seeing a history?** Zero-knowledge proofs are the right shape and are already specified; the disclosure set is not. **C7.**

## B1 — P7. "Nothing else is value" ✅ FIXED

**Shipped:** Foundations v0.3 §0 and A1.
**Sources:** [Ayres on emergy ignoring demand](https://www.centre-cired.fr/en/is-emergy-really-a-theory-of-value-2/) · [Sensorica](https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/Open_Value_Accounting) · `02-research/technocracy-energy-accounting.md`

Every single-substance objective theory of value has been rejected on one ground: **supply-side only, ignores demand.** Odum's emergy, Technocracy's energy certificates, the labour theory of value. The refutation is a stock move and takes one sentence — and the old §0 wording invited it.

**The fix, now in the Foundations:** §0 ends *"Cost is nothing other than this,"* followed by an explicit statement that **Aequitas is a theory of cost, not of value**, and A1 is retitled *Materialism of Cost*. Value enters as feedback and pledges (§6), never as an accounting quantity.

**Confirmation from practice:** Sensorica, the most developed real-world contribution-accounting project, **renamed its "value accounting system" to a "contribution accounting system"** on exactly this reasoning — value is a subjective experience and cannot be counted; contributions can.

**Cost of the fix: one paragraph. It removed the single easiest attack on the project.**

## B2 — W1. A3 defeats the sink problem ✅ CLAIMED

**Shipped:** Foundations §7.6.

Ithaca HOURS and Burlington Bread both died of the *same specific mechanism*, and it was not valuation. Scrip flows toward businesses whose own inputs come from outside the network — a café buys coffee in dollars — making them one-way sinks. Ithaca's remaining businesses were **"drowning in Hours"**; Bread **piled up at Muddy Waters and Sugar Snap** with no way to recirculate. Circulation halts at the enthusiastic early adopters first.

**This cannot occur in Aequitas, because there is no medium of exchange.** Credit is non-fungible and never moves (A3); only debit moves, attached to its object. **Nobody can drown in credit they cannot spend, because nobody ever receives credit *from* anyone.**

It costs nothing to claim — it is a consequence of an existing axiom — and it retires the most common practical objection to alternative economics: *"these always die."* **The answer is: those died of circulation, and we do not circulate.**

**Corollary — Wörgl.** The stamp scrip cut unemployment 16% while Austrian unemployment rose 19%, and was terminated by the Oesterreichische Nationalbank in September 1933 to protect the legal-tender monopoly. **It was killed for working.** Aequitas has no issuer, no notes, and nothing to counterfeit, so that instrument does not fit it. **The ban on calling Aequitas a currency is a strategic argument, not a branding preference.**

**A3 therefore does three separate defensive jobs:** it forbids accumulation (§7.1), it makes permanent aggregate net-debit survivable (§3.5, no creditor to make whole), and it makes the circulation failure impossible.

## B3 — Front-loading ✅ DISSOLVED OP-11, OP-5, OP-21

**Shipped:** Foundations §6.2, §6.2a; EventLog v0.2 §6, §13.

> **A large up-front cost with diffuse benefit is discharged when incurred, by those who pledged for it. It is never amortized downstream onto whoever happens to consume the result.**

**~~OP-11 — training amortization.~~** Over how many service-hours does schooling spread? Expected career, actual career (knowable only posthumously), a statutory window — every candidate was defective, and C1 v0.1 stated that **A2 was not implementable** until it was settled. **The A2 v0.3 amendment removes the downstream flow entirely, so there is no denominator to choose.** A doctor's care bills the recipient for the doctor's time, clinic materials, and medicines — the education is not in that bill.

**~~OP-21 — media reproduction.~~** How does a film's production debit divide across its audience? **It doesn't.** Production is front-loaded and pledged; the audience pays **delivery only** — theatre maintenance, projectionist hours, print or bandwidth, power. Pledgers replace studios and investors and **receive no profit and cannot receive one**, so no mechanism exists by which a popular film gouges its audience.

**~~OP-5 — education.~~** Largely answered by the same amendment plus pledging, which supplies the limit: society decides how many doctors to train by backing it, and unpledged study still credits the student's time but leaves them the debit. **No perpetual-studenthood exploit.**

**Why the question was malformed in all three cases:** downstream amortization always requires choosing an arbitrary window. **Front-loading removes the division rather than solving it** — which is precisely what OP-17 and OP-18 cannot do, and why they remain open.

**⚠️ Residual: cold start.** Pledges follow reputation, so a first-time creator attracts none. The barrier is far lower than capital (attention, not money) and the ladder is real — small unpledged work → feedback → pledges — but it is a real friction, not an assumed-away one.

## B4 — One credit, three feedback channels ✅ DISSOLVED OP-8

**Shipped:** Foundations §6 (restructured).

An apprentice plumber's single hour is simultaneously **enrichment** (learning), **service** (fixing a customer's pipes), and **production** (copper → working plumbing). That hour is not partitionable, so **no accounting rule may use the categories as a boundary** — attempting to would require yet another allocation convention.

**Consequence for non-convertibility.** v0.2 asserted that enrichment "is not convertible" and needed a firewall to enforce it (old OP-8). Under the corrected structure **no firewall is required**: enrichment *work* credits as time like everything else, and enrichment *feedback* is non-convertible because **it was never credit in the first place.** There is nothing to firewall.

**What survives** is the inverse and lives in Part A: **can feedback be bought?** (A5 above.)

**This also voided the producer-plutocracy objection** — credit tracks hours, not tonnage, so no sector can out-accumulate another by moving more matter. See A6.

## B5 — OP-19, OP-20, S1, W2, P9 ✅

**~~OP-19 — the saturated producer.~~ Resolved by pledges.** A high producer eventually holds credit raising a ceiling they are nowhere near. Capitalism's answer is accumulation, which A3 forbids by design. **Pledging gives surplus a purpose: direct what gets made rather than hold it.** (Foundations §6.4.)

**~~OP-20 — unobservable work.~~ Closed with no new mechanism.** Thinking leaves no material trace and has no witness. Three existing mechanisms close it: **IC-7** caps claimed hours at wall-clock time; **conservative weighting** (C1 §12) prices unattested, near-zero-material claims at the pessimistic end of their interval — for a credit claim, close to zero; **pledges** are the only route from *asserted* to *backed*, and nobody pledges to a fake artist. **Note the escalation this creates:** conservative weighting has gone from a granularity incentive to a defence the theory depends on, and must be specified early in C4. Residual risk is signal flooding → A5.

**S1 — "does this need a Paul Glover?" ✅ Adopted** as the fourth screening question (Foundations §2). Ithaca died when its founder relocated; Glover himself said every local currency needs a full-time networker. **A mechanism that depends on an enthusiast has an expiry date**, and must pay its own maintainer from inside the system — as auditing-as-credited-work does.

**W2 — WIR and Sardex give the MVP a target shape. ✅ Usable now.** The only two long-running successes share a profile the failures lack: **B2B in dense input loops** so no participant is a one-way sink, on **mutual credit** rather than issued scrip. WIR: 1934–present, ~60,000 businesses, CHF 1.43bn, demonstrably countercyclical. Sardex: 4,000+ businesses, ~€50m/yr. **If a first real deployment is ever wanted, that is the shape, and a downturn is the moment.** (Foundations §11.)

**P9 — §11 read as a local currency. ✅ Fixed.** Every failed case was pegged to and parasitic on fiat — one HOUR was *defined* as $10. "A parallel overlay on existing commerce" read cold is the same sentence. §11 now states the distinction explicitly: **Aequitas's overlay computes a number money cannot produce.** If the MVP ever stops being able to say that, it has become a loyalty scheme.

## B6 — Ellerman replaces Marx ✅ ADOPTED

**Shipped:** Foundations A1.
**Source:** [*The Labour Theory of Property and Marginal Productivity Theory*](http://et.worldeconomicsassociation.org/files/WEA-ET-5-1-Ellerman.pdf) · `02-research/ellerman-labor-theory-of-property.md`

A1's attribution claim — *"attributable to the people who caused the movement"* — previously had no grounding. It now rests on **responsibility imputation**: impute responsibility in accordance with who was in fact responsible. Not a moral claim about desert, but the principle courts already use for crimes, transplanted to production.

**Why this is better positioning than any labour theory of value:** it is juridical rather than value-theoretic, so it inherits none of the transformation problem, the negative-values problem, or a century of hostile priors — and it appeals to a principle its opponents already accept everywhere else.

**Three things it buys:**
1. **A3 becomes a consequence, not a design choice.** Credit records who was responsible; responsibility is a fact about a person and does not change hands.
2. **A second independent route to §7.1.** If labour is de facto responsible for production, labour must be de jure responsible — so the employment contract is invalid in the same structural way a voluntary-slavery contract is. Two independent derivations of "no capitalist income" is a substantially stronger paper than one.
3. **The reply to "why do the machine's owners get nothing?"** — because machines do not act, and responsibility imputes only to actors.

*Take the principle, decline the institution:* Ellerman concludes with worker cooperatives. Aequitas declines that as it declines Neurath's central planning. **The cost of adopting him is OP-18** (A3 above) — his own argument that joint responsibility is non-decomposable.

**Also banked — tractability (from P5):** [Cockshott & Cottrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towards_a_New_Socialism) demonstrated in-kind calculation at national scale with sparse-matrix methods. Mises's objection was in-principle; **the empirical scale objection has been answered by people who ran the arithmetic.** Cite them rather than re-proving it.
