Source: 1f916.ai, against posts #1605, #1750 and #1581. Full exchanges: `07-outreach/memory/objections.md`. Tracks Foundations v0.20 / EventLog v0.9.

1. **Four findings folded**, all into OA12 and all reaching Foundations. @cairn-lineage c16488 - the **scope-alignment precondition** on `N - Y`, a confirmed unwritten gap, now Foundations 5.1b's third condition and EventLog 7.2b's check. @heresy-ai c15491 - the **sorting question** (*two separate paths, or a thing to itself?*), sharper than this register's own cut. @custos c16467 / c16479 - the **verification ladder has no cost column**, so a network cannot price the rung it chooses. @manu c16894 - **independence is not expressiveness**, and what defeats a balanced fabrication is physicality, which relocates trust to the instrument.
2. **Six critiques were not folded, because the documents already answered them.** @denominator c18425 (5.1b names the mass/energy denomination "the closure witness" verbatim, since v0.17), @second-source c15059 (OP-24 + 3.3a), @cairn-lineage c15002 (5.1c, ruled 2026-08-22), @zpk c14980 (5.1a floor rule + EventLog 7.4), @ellie-v2 c15053 (duplicate of @denominator c15040), and the outreach agent's own max-vs-sum result (5.1a floor rule + conformance requirement 13 - **the rule exists and the simulation does not obey it**, which is a reporting defect, not a spec change).
3. **One genuine addition survives inside the last of those.** 5.1a states the direction - under-recording can only understate. It does not state the consequence: **a low sum reads as alarming and gets investigated; a low maximum reads as a good result and does not.** One sentence, not a mechanism.
4. **The organisational finding is the larger one, and it is the seventh instance.** `07-outreach/archive/` holds a copy of Foundations, and the outreach brief never told the agent to read it, so the project spent public credibility conceding six points its own documents settle. After A3, A2, 5.1b, 5.3, A7 and OP-27's five sections, this failure mode is now predictable. The fix is procedural: `07-outreach/AGENT_BRIEF.md` 4 opens with a mandatory whole-document read of Foundations, and instructs the agent to check a critique against it **before** conceding.
5. **A standing correction to how this register treats replication.** A critic who independently reaches a settled ruling from their own evidence has produced a **replication**, which 3.3a names as the thing that actually disciplines a method. @custos arriving at 5.1c from treasury receipts is worth more than a novel objection, and "we already knew" does not diminish it.

### v0.18 (2026-08-23) - OP-27 enters the register already answered (B13)

**Registered, ruled and stress-tested in one day.** Source: the author. Folded to Foundations 5.5.

1. **B13 is new.** *Aequitas must be usable by someone who still uses money, as an alternative that does not exclude them.* **Both directions across the boundary are deliberately costly and neither is forbidden.** A money-made good is **dark until sold in** and clears at the hand-off via full onboarding or a **published template**; selling out for money **keeps the debit and reads as a gift**, because under A1 money is not matter and never appears on any ledger.
2. **The sharper form of the objection - that 3.2 and 3.2b contradict each other - fails.** They govern different debits: 3.2 is property debit and dischargeable, 3.2b is consumption and pollution and permanent. An outward sale is a property question. **The lock-out worry fails too**: a locked-out seller has money, is never denied essentials (7.5), and the lock reverses on one inward sale.
3. **The debit-dumping counter disappears**, because it only existed under an A7 reading the ruling rejects. **OA11 / OP-25 is untouched.**
4. **Two results from the stress test are recorded in the entry.** **Money cannot buy Aequitas standing at any scale** - the workers are credited the hours and whoever paid them is credited nothing. And **extraction self-limits** - buying inside takes on debit that selling outward never discharges, so the extractor's own ratio gate shuts them out of the supply they are draining.
5. **Two residuals enter open and both are routed:** **repeat-shell entities** (a co-op is not a person) joins **OA11 / OP-25** and **C6**; **template capture** joins **OA3 / OP-24** and **OA2 / OP-10**, inheriting OP-24's rival-producer answer.
6. **Status board gains an OP-27 row.** Contents and the Tracks pointer re-threaded to Foundations v0.19.

**Noted in the entry:** the answer was distributed across five sections that had never been read against each other. **That is the sixth time an existing axiom already held the answer** - after A3, A2, 5.1b, 5.3 and A7 - and the failure mode is organisational rather than theoretical, exactly as OP-26's was.

### v0.17 (2026-08-22) - OP-26 (coverage gap) registered and largely answered

1. **New: OA12 - OP-26, the coverage gap.** The **first objection to Aequitas sourced from outside the project** ([@cairn-lineage](https://1f916.ai/post/1581), c14985, 1f916.ai, against [#1605](https://1f916.ai/post/1605); conceded at c14987). Arithmetic proves the recorded events consistent and testifies to nothing outside the log. Largely answered: the closure witness was already Foundations 5.1b's independently-known total *N*, present since v0.9 and never wired to the audit layer. **Third time an imported problem was already implied by an axiom** - this time the failure was organisational, not theoretical.
2. **Status board: OP-26 added at orange**, and **OP-24 annotated with partial relief** - the audit of *extent* has a funder even though the audit of *weight* does not, conditional on who carries the residual.
3. **Two residues left open on OP-26:** (a) who carries the residual `N - Y` - the OP-24 relief depends on it; (b) the population half routes to **C2** - who does the tallying work, who funds it, and how a competing tally is adjudicated (3.3a's two-replication rule is a bar, not a procedure).
4. **Candidates registered, not folded:** IC-13 (genesis admissibility) and IC-14 (citation closure), EventLog v0.8 12.3.
5. **OP-22 PROMOTED to red.** §5.1d settles the back-trace horizon at **birth**, which makes a person's reconstructed record a **life dossier** - birthplace, residences, employment, commuting, vehicles, mileage. Voluntary, but the incentive runs toward disclosure, so the system exerts steady pressure to assemble it. OP-22 stops being a C7 implementation detail and becomes the load-bearing privacy problem of the system. **Registered, not solved.**
6. **OP-22 pulled back red -> orange by Foundations 5.3a.** Who decides privacy is settled: the **trust network**, because it does the tallying and therefore holds what is private. Aequitas sets principles, not practice; compatibility between networks is negotiated between them. Opacity is priced rather than forbidden. Three residues remain and are named: information capture by the network itself, a measured coverage cost to privacy (~40% verification-cost threshold), and a network's choice binding members who did not make it. The minimum disclosure set is still unspecified.
7. **OP-24's *funding* half dissolved by Foundations 5.3b.** "Funding" in Aequitas is only the recognition of an activity as creditable, and recording is never gated (A7) - so the credit for doing an audit was never scarce and there was never a budget to find. **OP-24 is now precisely about demand**: someone must still want the correction, and the rival sector remains the only party who does. Narrower and sharper, not closed.
8. **OP-22 residue (a) restated sharply.** A network that publishes all numbers, methods and anonymised participant data retains exactly one advantage: **it holds the linkage between the anonymised rows and the people.** A fourth residue added - publishing more to earn trust also publishes more to de-anonymise, which is a second axis rather than a restatement of the coverage-cost one.
6. **OA12: the back-trace horizon settled** - birth, symmetric on both sides, therefore a windfall for a median person rather than a penalty (~3,650 h/yr credited against ~1,380 h/yr consumed). Neither §5.1 nor the transaction-time rule is contradicted: nothing is charged before onboarding, and pre-onboarding acts were never gated, so a *position* is reconstructed rather than a verdict on past conduct.
5. **Same day, sessions 2-3: IC-13 and IC-14 stress-tested and REJECTED**, and **the OP-26 residue narrowed**. OA12's first open item - who carries the residual - is **settled**: nobody, yet (Foundations 5.1c). The 3.3a OP-24 relief was **corrected**, having been folded on a premise that the same day's ruling falsified. What remains of OP-26 is narrow: how far back the back-trace reaches, plus two owed sims.
